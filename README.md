## Creating a Ticket in ServiceNow

### Objective

This SOP outlines the steps to create a ticket in ServiceNow for incidents, ensuring consistency and accuracy in reporting issues.

### Key Steps

 

**Step 1: Log into ServiceNow** [0:01](https://loom.com/share/6eeee2e71dbd4d1cbff5ce51474e541a?t=1)

![log into servicenow](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/76796be0-3a75-425a-80bd-031addbc33c3)


- Access the ServiceNow platform by entering your credentials.
- Ensure you are logged in successfully.

 

**Step 2: Navigate to Incidents** [0:01](https://loom.com/share/6eeee2e71dbd4d1cbff5ce51474e541a?t=1)

![navigate to Incident](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4b42ca35-417d-45b0-8288-4dbb5be8400f)


- Once logged in, locate the menu on the left side.
- Click on 'All' to expand the menu options.
- Scroll down and select 'Incidents' from the list.

 

**Step 3: Select Incident Type** [0:34](https://loom.com/share/6eeee2e71dbd4d1cbff5ce51474e541a?t=34)

![Incident Type](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cf1e741c-7de8-434e-be3a-4920f53a8d90)


- In the incidents section, you will see various options for incident types.
- Choose the appropriate incident type based on the issue you are reporting (e.g., 'Test Pass for Lockout').
